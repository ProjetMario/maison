Lake-view house for sale in Voglans

€780,000 · 133 m² · 3 bedrooms · DPE B

A contemporary home overlooking Lac du Bourget and the Dent du Chat, with a heated pool, three terraces and a double garage. Direct sale by the owners.

Living spaces designed around the view

Built in 2020, the house has an inverted layout: the open-plan kitchen and living room are upstairs, while the bedrooms are on the lower level. The living space opens onto the terraces and garden. There are stairs, so this is not a step-free property.

The lower level includes 3 bedrooms, including a main bedroom with a dressing area and private shower room. There is also a laundry room, another bathroom with a bath and shower, and separate toilets.

Outdoor spaces and equipment

The house has a heated saltwater pool, a timber terrace, a south-facing tiled terrace and a north-facing terrace accessible from the living room. Underfloor heating is supplied by a heat pump, and the living room has air conditioning.

A double garage and additional parking complete the property. The garage shares a wall with the neighbouring garage. An electric vehicle charging point is listed among the equipment; its details can be checked during a viewing.

Location and points to consider

The address is 93 chemin de la Combe, 73420 Voglans, Savoie. The house has a view of the lake, not a private waterfront or direct access to its shores. You will need to travel to a public beach or lakeside access.

The owners report some overlooking and possible noise from the main road and airport. Please assess these during your visit, alongside the stairs and shared garage wall. Explore the location and prepare your viewing.
