Enjoying the lake

The house overlooks Lac du Bourget but is not a waterfront property. Public beaches and lakeside walks are separate destinations. Les Mottets beach is in nearby Viviers-du-Lac; check Grand Lac’s beach information for access conditions, fees and seasonal supervision.
